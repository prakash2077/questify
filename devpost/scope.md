# Solo Leveling-Style Life App (working title)

A daily-quest app that gamifies your real life like *Solo Leveling*: finish your tasks, grow your shadow army; miss them, and the app locks into a Penalty Zone.

## The Unique Kernel
The **Penalty Zone**. When you miss a task, the rest of the app shuts until you clear a hard penalty task you chose in advance. That screen doesn't just punish. It gently shows you the full picture: your goal, how many weeks you have left in your life, and a real, influential quote. The aim is to motivate without being harsh, so people don't just uninstall.

> "Instead of facing the penalty I would try to do the task... it should be somewhat harder... but gentle, something that feels awesome and heartwarming."

## Who It's For
A student like the learner: someone who wants to improve their life and achieve goals, likely a *Solo Leveling* fan, who starts the day wanting structure. Today they have no good plan, and apps that rely on willpower get skipped or uninstalled.

## The Core Loop
Open the app each morning and see today's quests, with deadlines and priority. Start a task, which opens a focused black screen showing only the task name and a fire symbol. Finish it and check it off to earn coins. Spend the coins on weapons and shadow soldiers (named by you) that grow your army. Miss a task and the Penalty Zone locks everything until you clear it. You come back because the army grows and the penalty is unavoidable.

## Inspiration & Identity
- *Solo Leveling* (the System, the Penalty Zone, the shadow army), *Demon Slayer*.
- Clash of Clans-style building: easy to understand, and watching it grow feels good.
- Dark, AMOLED-friendly, true-black look with a motivating fire symbol; cute, game-like and unique, not generic.
- Cinematic first: just using the app should give a dopamine hit and a feeling of progress. (The build's first, cute cartoon look was rejected in review as too childish; the look is now dark and sleek.)
- Sound effects are important. Key moments (check-off, coins, a new soldier, the Penalty Zone) should sound good, so it feels like a game.
- Fonts and styling close to the *Solo Leveling* System UI in the anime, but with a "fire plus cool" Pinterest-style vibe.
- Real, influential quotes. Heartwarming, not harsh.

## Why This Matters to the Learner
The learner is on a dopamine detox, working on self-improvement, and wants to be stable enough to take care of family. They want a "level up" system for their own life that actually looks good, and they want to describe it and have the AI build it.

## What "Working" Looks Like
The learner opens the app and sees today's quests with deadlines and priority. They start a task and get the black focus screen with the task name and fire. They check it off and earn coins, then buy a soldier, name it, and see the army grow. The "oh, that's cool" beat: a missed task locks the app into the Penalty Zone, with their goal, weeks left, and a quote.

## The POC Boundary
- Quest list with deadlines and user-set priority.
- Check off a task; focused black screen with task name and fire symbol.
- Coins for finished tasks.
- A simple empire/army, made concrete: a shop where coins buy a weapon or a soldier; you name each soldier; a single army screen shows every soldier you own, so it visibly gets bigger. Easy enough for a beginner to understand at a glance. (Proposed from the learner's description; confirm in review.)
- Sound effects on key moments, and a cinematic, cute look and feel.
- Missed task leads to the Penalty Zone: app locked, pre-chosen hard penalty, goal, weeks left, and a quote.
- A phone-sized web app (stands in for the phone experience).

## Later
- Mini-games.
- AI-assigned priority.
- Phone home-screen widget.
- Clash of Clans-style attacks with your army.
- Online challenges against other people, winning their soldiers.

## Explicitly Cut
- Real-life offers and rewards: depends on outside partners, not needed to prove the kernel.
- Progress-as-stake losses (losing rank/level on a skip): the learner prefers the Penalty Zone approach.
