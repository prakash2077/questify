Solo Leveling-Style Life App

Working title. A phone-sized web app: finish daily quests, grow your shadow army; miss one and the Penalty Zone locks everything. Canonical text lives in prd.md.

Scope said → Now we know

Scope said
  • “Quest list with deadlines and priority”
  • “Missed task leads to the Penalty Zone”
  • “A simple empire/army”
Now
  • Goals hold daily quests entered once; max 3 a day; each has a pre-chosen penalty
  • Missed = deadline passes; photo proof clears it; weeks-left grid + goal + quote
  • Full-screen 2D battleground: fire, named soldiers, demons that grow with level

Look and Feel

  • True-black AMOLED, Solo Leveling System-style type; blue by default, red in the Penalty Zone; accent colour switchable in Settings.
  • Sleek short 3D-feeling intro; a real burning bonfire (building something, cooking something); thick clean Duolingo-style 2D sprites drawn from scratch.
  • Sound at key moments; the battleground gets richer in sound as the army grows. Heartwarming, not harsh.
