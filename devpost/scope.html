Solo Leveling-Style Life App (working title)

A daily-quest app that gamifies your real life: finish tasks and grow your shadow army; miss them and the app locks into a Penalty Zone.

The Unique Kernel

The Penalty Zone. Miss a task and the rest of the app shuts until you clear a hard penalty you chose in advance. The screen also gently shows the full picture: your goal, the weeks you have left in your life, and a real, influential quote. It motivates without being harsh, so people don't just uninstall.
"It should be somewhat harder... but gentle, something that feels awesome and heartwarming."

The Core Loop

You come back because the army grows, and the penalty is unavoidable.

Who It's For & Why

Who
A student like you who wants to improve and reach goals, probably a Solo Leveling fan. Today there's no good plan, and apps that rely on willpower get skipped or uninstalled.
Why it matters
You're on a dopamine detox, working on self-improvement, and want to be stable enough to take care of family. You want a level-up system for your own life.

Look & Feel

Solo Leveling and Demon Slayer energy. A true-black AMOLED look with a grinding fire symbol. Cinematic and cute at once, so just using it gives a dopamine hit and a sense of progress. Sound effects matter: check-offs, coins, new soldiers and the Penalty Zone should sound good, like a game. Fonts and styling close to the anime's System UI, with a "fire plus cool" Pinterest vibe. Heartwarming, not harsh.

What "Working" Looks Like

Open the app and see today's quests with deadlines and priority. Start a task and get the black focus screen with the task name and fire. Check it off, earn coins, buy and name a soldier, and watch the army grow.

The "oh, that's cool" beat: a missed task locks the app into the Penalty Zone, with your goal, weeks left, and a quote.

Boundary

  • The empire, made concrete: a shop where coins buy a weapon or a soldier, you name each soldier, and one army screen shows everything you own so it visibly grows. Easy to understand at a glance. (Proposed from your description; confirm in review.)
  • Sound effects on key moments; cinematic, cute look and feel
  • Missed task opens the Penalty Zone: app locked, pre-chosen hard penalty, goal, weeks left, quote

Cut: and why
